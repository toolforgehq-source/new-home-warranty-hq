# Builder warranty request letter templates

Templates are educational samples only. They are not legal advice and may not meet your state's notice requirements or your specific warranty contract.

## How to use
- Replace bracketed fields. Attach dated photos.
- Send by the method your warranty docs require (portal, email, certified mail). Keep your own copy.
- These are sample letters, not legal forms. State notice rules vary.
- Pair with the [11-month warranty inspection checklist](/guides/11-month-checklist) and [what to do when your builder warranty is expiring](/guides/builder-warranty-expiring).

---

### Sample 1: Initial written warranty request (email or letter)

Subject: Warranty request - [Property address] - [Closing date or warranty start]

[Date]

[Builder company name]
[Warranty / customer care email or address]

Re: Builder warranty request for [full property address]

Hello,

I am the homeowner at [address], closed on [closing date]. Under my builder warranty, I am reporting the following items for repair or correction:

1. [Room/location]: [Short description]. First noticed: [date]. Photos: [filenames or "attached"].
2. [Room/location]: [Short description]. First noticed: [date]. Photos: attached.
3. [Room/location]: [Short description]. First noticed: [date]. Photos: attached.

Please confirm receipt of this request in writing and provide the expected inspection or repair timeline. You may reach me at [phone] or [email].

I will keep a personal copy of this notice and the attached photos for my records.

Thank you,
[Full name]
[Mailing address if different]
[Phone]
[Email]

### Sample 2: Follow-up after no response

Subject: Follow-up - Warranty request [date of first notice] - [Property address]

[Date]

[Builder company name]

Re: Follow-up on warranty request dated [first notice date]

Hello,

On [first notice date] I submitted a written warranty request for [address] covering [N] items (copy attached). I have not received a written confirmation or schedule.

Please reply within [reasonable period you choose, e.g. 7 or 10 business days] with:
- Confirmation this request is open
- Planned inspection or repair date(s)
- Any additional information you need from me

I remain ready to provide access with reasonable notice.

Thank you,
[Full name]
[Phone]
[Email]

---

### Sample 3: Coverage window ending soon

Subject: Time-sensitive warranty notice - coverage ending [end date] - [Property address]

[Date]

[Builder company name]

Re: Warranty items requiring attention before [coverage end date]

Hello,

My [workmanship / applicable] warranty coverage for [address] is scheduled to end on or about [end date], based on [closing package / warranty booklet citation if known].

Open items previously reported on [prior notice date(s)]:
1. [Item] - status: [no reply / inspected / partial repair]
2. [Item] - status: …

New items documented on [date(s)]:
1. [Item + photo reference]
2. [Item + photo reference]

Please confirm in writing that these items are accepted for warranty handling before [coverage end date], and provide the repair plan. I am available for access on [date options].

Thank you,
[Full name]
[Phone]
[Email]

## Optional short portal paste version
Use when the builder portal has a small text box:

"Homeowner [name], [address], closed [date]. Warranty items: (1) [location] - [issue] - noticed [date]; (2) … Photos uploaded. Please confirm receipt and repair timeline in writing to [email]."

## Tips that help (non-legal)
- One issue per bullet; include room and date noticed
- Photos with a ruler or coin for scale when useful
- Save PDF/EML exports from portals; do not rely on the portal alone
- Calendar a follow-up before any stated deadline

## Disclaimer
Templates are educational samples only. They are not legal advice and may not meet your state's notice requirements or your specific warranty contract. Read your warranty booklet and purchase agreement. For disputes or safety issues, consult a licensed professional or attorney in your state.
