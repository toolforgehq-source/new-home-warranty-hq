# 11-month warranty inspection checklist

## How to use this checklist
- Best window: months 9–11 after closing (confirm your builder warranty dates from your closing package).
- Walk the home room by room. Note location, photo, and date for every item.
- Book a licensed inspector if you want a third-party report; this list is for DIY capture or prep for that visit.
- Send written notice to your builder before coverage windows close. Keep your own copy.

Letter templates: [Builder warranty request letter templates](/guides/builder-warranty-letters).

## Exterior
- [ ] Roof: missing/loose shingles, flashing at chimneys/vents, staining under eaves
- [ ] Gutters/downspouts: leaks, standing water at foundation, splash blocks
- [ ] Siding/trim: gaps, warping, unfinished edges, sealant cracks
- [ ] Windows/doors (outside): caulk gaps, weatherstripping, water stains on frames
- [ ] Grading/drainage: soil slopes toward foundation; soft spots; poor drainage after rain
- [ ] Driveway/walks/patio: settlement cracks, trip hazards, incomplete sealing (if required)
- [ ] Deck/porch/stairs: loose rails, soft boards, improper ledger attachment (visual only)
- [ ] Exterior outlets/fixtures: covers, GFCI where expected, working lights
- [ ] Hose bibs: leaks, proper shutoff, winterization notes if applicable

## Garage
- [ ] Door opener and safety reverse
- [ ] Weather seal at door bottom and sides
- [ ] Floor cracks, settlement, drain function (if present)
- [ ] Firewall/separation to living space: penetrations, unfinished drywall seams (visual)

## Entry, halls, stairs
- [ ] Door hardware, locks, thresholds, sticking doors
- [ ] Flooring transitions and trip edges
- [ ] Handrails secure; stair nosings consistent
- [ ] Smoke/CO detectors present and tested (replace batteries if needed)

## Kitchen
- [ ] Cabinets/drawers: alignment, soft-close (if specified), hardware secure
- [ ] Countertops: seams, chips, caulk to backsplash
- [ ] Sink/faucet: leaks under sink, supply lines, disposal mount
- [ ] Appliances (builder-supplied): operation per manual; note remaining manufacturer warranties separately
- [ ] Backsplash/grout: cracks, incomplete seal
- [ ] Vent hood ducting: draws air; exterior termination clear

## Bathrooms
- [ ] Tub/shower: grout/caulk, leaks at valves, loose fixtures
- [ ] Toilet: stable, no rocking, supply line dry
- [ ] Vanity/sink: leaks, drain slow, caulk gaps
- [ ] Exhaust fan: operates; vents outside (not into attic) if accessible to verify
- [ ] GFCI outlets trip/reset correctly
- [ ] Mirror/hardware secure; water damage on walls/ceiling

## Living areas / bedrooms
- [ ] Drywall: nail pops, cracks at joints/corners, unfinished texture
- [ ] Paint/stain: missed spots, peeling, inconsistent finish on trim
- [ ] Floors: soft spots, uneven transitions, gaps, cupping
- [ ] Windows: open/lock, fogged insulated glass, screens
- [ ] Closets: shelving secure, doors aligned
- [ ] Ceiling fans/lights: secure, wobble, switches labeled

## Utility / mechanical
- [ ] HVAC: heating and cooling cycle; filter access; unusual noise; condensate drain clear
- [ ] Water heater: T&P discharge path, drip pan (if required), leaks at fittings
- [ ] Electrical panel: labeled breakers (visual); no burning smell; do not remove deadfront
- [ ] Plumbing: visible supply/drain leaks; water pressure anomalies; whole-house shutoff known
- [ ] Attic (if safely accessible): insulation gaps, moisture stains, bath fan ducts, baffles at eaves
- [ ] Crawlspace/basement (if present): moisture, standing water, vapor barrier, pest signs

## Performance checks (short tests)
- [ ] Run water in all fixtures 2–3 minutes; check for leaks below
- [ ] Flush every toilet; listen for continuous fill
- [ ] Test all exterior and bath GFCI
- [ ] After rain (or hose test carefully): re-check window sills and basement/crawl for intrusion
- [ ] Note rooms that are much hotter/colder than others

## Before you notify the builder
1. Confirm which coverage window applies (often 1-year workmanship; systems and structural may differ).
2. Prefer written notice with photos and dates; keep a personal copy outside any builder portal.
3. Ask for expected response timeline in writing.
4. Calendar a follow-up before the coverage end date.

See also: [Builder Warranty vs Home Warranty: What's the Difference for New Construction?](/guides/builder-warranty-vs-home-warranty).

## Disclaimer
This checklist is general information for homeowners, not an inspection report, building code opinion, or legal advice. Follow your purchase agreement, builder warranty documents, and local rules. When in doubt, hire a licensed inspector or qualified contractor.
