# Builder Warranty Expiring? Your 30-Day Plan

Coverage on most components usually expires after the first year, per the FTC; HVAC, plumbing and electrical are generally two years. Your document sets the real dates.

**Day 1: Confirm the dates.** Find the start date and the end date for each component. If unclear, ask the builder in writing.
**Days 2-5: Walk every room.** Use our free checklist ([/guides/11-month-checklist](/guides/11-month-checklist)). Photograph each issue with a date.
**Days 3-7: Consider a pro.** A professional warranty inspection can find more (see [/guides/11-month-warranty-inspection](/guides/11-month-warranty-inspection)). Booking takes time.
**Days 5-10: Submit in writing.** Follow the warranty's method. The FTC suggests certified mail with return receipt for proof. Templates: [/guides/builder-warranty-letters](/guides/builder-warranty-letters).
**Days 10-30: Follow up and keep records.** Log replies and repair dates; re-submit in writing if a deadline is near.

## If the deadline already passed
Ask the builder in writing anyway and check for longer coverage (2-year systems, structural). Questions about your rights belong with a licensed attorney in your state or your state/local builders' board.

## Don't repeat this scramble
Calendar reminders at 60 and 14 days before each window; or use New Home Warranty HQ ($189 one-time), which provides reminders based on terms you confirm.
