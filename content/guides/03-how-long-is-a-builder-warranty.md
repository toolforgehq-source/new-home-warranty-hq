# How Long Is a Builder Warranty and What Does It Cover?

Builder warranties vary, so your warranty document is the final word. The FTC describes this common pattern:

- **1 year:** workmanship and materials on most components, for example siding and stucco, doors and trim, drywall and paint.
- **2 years:** HVAC, plumbing and electrical systems are generally covered for two years.
- **Up to 10 years:** some builders cover "major structural defects" (the FTC's example: a roof that could collapse).

## Usually not covered (per FTC)
- Household appliances
- Small cracks in brick, tile, cement or drywall
- Components under a manufacturer's warranty
- Out-of-pocket costs such as moving out during repairs

## How to read yours
1. Find the start date (closing date? possession date? Check the document).
2. List each coverage period by component.
3. Note how claims must be submitted (form, portal, email, mail) and any notice requirements.
4. Note dispute steps: the FTC says many warranties offer or require mediation and arbitration.
5. Put the dates on your calendar. Our checklist and letters help: [/guides/11-month-checklist](/guides/11-month-checklist) and [/guides/builder-warranty-letters](/guides/builder-warranty-letters).
